## Context

Fabien asked for the exhaustive list of communities Ouch! could propose and reach, and which are the most relevant. The wiki already fixes the frame: one community at a time (D3), a community is a `communities` tag and a curated deck on its own page (dedup spec), each community lives on several channel types (playbook), and the next community is only opened at day 90 with the replay kit. This page is the **long list plus the ranking** that the day-90 decision and the Phase 2 channel waves need. Nothing here re-opens D1–D6. Scores are the team's judgement, not measurements; the Sunday sheet will correct them once real `?c=` numbers exist.

## 1. What makes a community "good" for Ouch! — six criteria (0–3 each, max 18)

| Code | Criterion | 3 means | 0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** | Reach | dense online gathering places (groups, Slack/Discord, forums) where a member post is tolerated, and Fabien can get in | scattered audience, no member posting, no access |
| **P** | Pain density | recurring, concrete frictions that fit one first-person sentence with a number | diffuse dissatisfaction, needs an essay |
| **M** | Maker value | Type A share high, the person buys tools alone and fast, makers already sell to them | Type B only, nothing a third-party maker can sell |
| **E** | Entity richness | many real, sourceable organisations to name (entity pages, SEO surface) | placeholders only, or unnameable counterparts |
| **S** | Safety | facts stay administrative and non-divisive, playful register possible, no sensitive data category | policy divisiveness, health/immigration/dignity issues, defamation exposure |
| **F** | Fit with the loop now | same channel types and expert skill as the freelance iteration, timely trigger, replay kit reusable | needs a new expert, new channels, or is the mainstream launch the plan forbids |

Rule of thumb: **S ≤ 1 excludes**, whatever the total. R and M weigh most for the cold start (both sides of the market must exist).

## 3. Ranking and recommendation

### Now, weeks 4–13 (no new deck, per D3 and the feature freeze)

The freelance community is one tag but at least three **distribution sub-profiles**, each with its own channels and `?c=`. This is where "several communities" is real today without touching the product:

The fourth row is the cheapest two-sided move available: a solopreneur who swipes a Stripe payout card is the same person who opens the Terminal a week later.

### Day 90 — Cercle 2 shortlist (pick one, build the replay kit on it)

Four candidates tie at 16/18; reach (R) and maker value (M) break the tie because a cold start needs both sides in the first month.

1. **Independent hosts (#15)** — R3 M3, large host groups, platform entities (Airbnb, Booking) plus town halls, and possibly Fabien's own network. **Rank 1 only if direct access is confirmed**; otherwise it drops to 3.
2. **E-merchants (#5)** — R3 M3, three named FB groups plus the official Amazon/Etsy forums as harvest sources; same mechanics as the freelance platform cards (Malt, Qonto, Stripe) with Amazon/Etsy/Colissimo. Default pick if #15 has no warm access.
3. **Office managers / HR in SMEs (#14)** — the truest answer to "Cercle 2 = PME" (the PME is reached through the person who swipes, not the owner who does not) and the safest register, but the online communities are small (Slack by co-optation, FB groups of a few hundred): a warm-intro play, slower than a group post.
4. **Associations (#17)** — the alternate if civic-but-safe content is what the home deck needs; large mutual-aid groups, playful register works, no divisive edge.

Before any of these: **trainers and coaches (#6)** are still independents, hence Cercle 1, and the search found three FB groups of 6–8k members each. They are the cheapest "second community" because they need no new tag, no new page and no day-90 gate: a second curated deck row on `/communaute/independants` and their own `?c=`.

Accountants (#16) are not a deck but a **weekly harvest source** for both freelance and SME cards from week 6.

### Later — Cercle 3 order

Parents (#21) → students (#22) → travellers as viral content (#23). Everything scored S ≤ 1 stays out until `ouch-legal` writes a frame for it.

- Should the Cercle 3 lead (parents) be validated by a domain seat in the panel before the day-90 review, the way freelances got `ouch-expert-independants`?
