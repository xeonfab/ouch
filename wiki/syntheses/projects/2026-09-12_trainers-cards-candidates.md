# Trainers & coaches sub-profile — fact-check of the mechanics and 10 candidate cards

> One-line TL;DR: `ouch-expert-independants` checked the Qualiopi / CPF / OPCO / BPF / VAT mechanics against dated sources before any trainer card is written. Two of them are **timely pain spikes**: the CPF holder's mandatory contribution rose to 150 € on 2 April 2026, and OPCO payment subrogation ends on 1 October 2026 (private sources only, official confirmation pending). Ten candidate cards follow, in the deck CSV format, two of them naming an entity (Mon Compte Formation, France Compétences) with their sourcing rows logged.

## Context

The communities map found trainers and coaches to be the densest untouched Cercle 1 sub-profile (three Facebook groups of 6–8k members each) and made an expert fact-check the precondition for writing cards. This page is that fact-check plus the first ten candidate cards. The cards are **not inserted**: they go through `ouch-editeur-cartes` for the dedup query and through Fabien for the go, like the 54 freelance cards.

## 1. Mechanics verified (dated)

| Mechanism | What is true on 2026-09-12 | Source | Card-worthy? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Qualiopi** | Mandatory since 1 Jan 2022 for any public or mutualised funding (OPCO, CPF, France Travail, Régions). Certificate valid 3 years; **surveillance audit between month 14 and 22**; renewal audit between month 24 and 36. Order of magnitude for a solo trainer: initial audit 600–1 200 € HT, surveillance 400–900 € HT, about 2 500 € HT per 3-year cycle | [ICPF](https://certif-icpf.org/articles/comment-se-passe-laudit-de-surveillance-qualiopi), [Procelio](https://procelio.fr/blog/preparer-audit-qualiopi), [Kaliopi](https://kaliopi.io/guides/cout-certification-qualiopi-2026) | Yes: the ongoing proof-keeping between audits (Type A, tools exist). The cost alone is "felt injustice", not a card |
| **CPF sub-contracting** | Décret n° 2023-1350 of 28 Dec 2023: since 1 April 2024 a sub-contractor on CPF actions must hold Qualiopi, **except micro-social businesses under 77 700 € HT** turnover; written contract mandatory; an OF may sub-contract at most 80 % of its CPF turnover | [Certifopac](https://certifopac.fr/qualiopi/financeurs/cpf/sous-traitance/), [Qualiobee](https://www.qualiobee.fr/blog/sous-traitant-qualiopi), [Queoval](https://www.queoval-formation.com/actualites/decryptage-du-decret-cpf-sous-traitance-et-qualiopi) | Yes: one compliance file per ordering OF (Type A) |
| **CPF holder contribution** | Mandatory participation of the holder, indexed: 103,20 € in early 2026, **150 € since 2 April 2026** (décret n° 2026-234 of 30 March 2026). Exempt: compensated jobseekers, holders with an employer top-up. The OF cannot pay it for the holder | [Via Compétences](https://www.via-competences.fr/actualites/cpf-participation-forfaitaire-en-hausse-en-2026), [Centre Inffo](https://www.centre-inffo.fr/site-droit-formation/actualites-droit/cpf-hausse-du-montant-de-la-participation-forfaitaire-du-titulaire), [moncompteformation.gouv.fr](https://www.moncompteformation.gouv.fr/espace-public/une-participation-financiere-pourquoi-et-comment) | Yes, **timely**: conversion drop on CPF sales since April (Type A: employer top-up workflows exempt the holder) |
| **CPF payment by Caisse des Dépôts** | After the OF declares exit and *service fait*, validation is automatic within 5 working days (unless control), then payment within **30 calendar days** of a compliant request, plus bank transfer time | [of.moncompteformation.gouv.fr — délais](https://of.moncompteformation.gouv.fr/aide/quels-sont-les-delais-de-paiement-de-la-caisse-des-depots), [service fait](https://of.moncompteformation.gouv.fr/espace-public/aide/comment-declarer-le-service-fait-qui-valide-le-service-fait) | Yes (Type B, entity `mon-compte-formation`) |
| **OPCO file and payment** | Proof of completion required (art. R6313-3 Code du travail): attendance sheets, *certificat de réalisation*, invoice; processing 15–30 working days depending on the OPCO; any missing or non-compliant document rejects the file and **restarts the clock** | [Opco Atlas](https://www.opco-atlas.fr/prestataire/actions-formation-et-pieces-justificatives-obligatoires.html), [Fresh Management](https://fresh-management.fr/subrogation-paiement-opco-organisme-formation/), [Mon Budget OPCO](https://mon-budget-opco.fr/info-pratique/news-opco/certificat-realisation-opco-mentions-obligatoires-2026) | Yes (Type A, no entity: "OPCO" is a category of 11 bodies) |
| **End of OPCO subrogation** | Several training-sector sources state that payment subrogation ends on **1 October 2026** for almost all schemes: the client company pays the OF and is reimbursed by its OPCO afterwards | [Certiforma](https://certiforma.fr/fin-subrogation-opco-octobre-2026/), [Mill Forma](https://mill-forma.fr/blog/fin-subrogation-opco-2026/), [Mesformations](https://mesformations-business.com/fin-subrogation-opco-ce-qui-change/) | Yes, **timely**, but **private sources only**: `ouch-legal` to confirm on an official text before publication |
| **Bilan pédagogique et financier (BPF)** | Annual, on « Mon Activité Formation »; regulatory deadline 30 April, campaign 2026 ran 1 April to **31 May 2026** (extension granted as in previous years). Missing it makes the activity declaration (NDA) lapse | [Via Compétences](https://www.via-competences.fr/actualites/bpf-2026-jusqua-quand-pour-le-remplir), [DREETS Bretagne](https://bretagne.dreets.gouv.fr/Campagne-2026-Depot-de-votre-Bilan-pedagogique-et-financier-BPF), [Liaisons sociales](https://www.liaisons-sociales.fr/lsq/2026/03/27/les-organismes-de-formation-doivent-transmettre-leur-bilan-pedagogique-et-financier-entre-le-1er-avril-et-le-31-mai-2026) | Yes (Type A, spring calendar card) |
| **VAT exemption for training** | Not automatic: art. 261-4-4°-a CGI exemption requires a tax attestation requested from the DREETS (form 3511) after the NDA. Widely believed to be automatic | expert knowledge, impots.gouv.fr / BOFiP to be linked at insertion | Yes, as an "information hard to find" card, not a mere misunderstanding |
| **CPF eligibility** | A CPF-listed action must lead to a certification registered at RNCP or RS (France Compétences) or be one of the listed exceptions; independents without their own certification buy a *habilitation* from a certifier | francecompetences.fr (to link at insertion) | Yes (Type B, entity `france-competences`) |

Rule applied: no regulatory amount inside a card unless it is the lived fact itself (the 150 € contribution is what the trainee pays, hence allowed); all other figures stay in this sheet with their date.

## 2. Trainer pain calendar (where the cards sit)

| Moment | Friction |
|---|---|
| Any sale to a company | five documents before a date is confirmed; OPCO funding file; from October 2026 the client advances the price |
| End of each session | attendance sheets, certificat de réalisation, service fait on EDOF, then 5 + 30 days of waiting |
| Between audits | proof-keeping for 32 Qualiopi indicators, surveillance audit around month 18 |
| April–May | BPF on Mon Activité Formation |
| First year | NDA, VAT attestation, certification or habilitation for CPF |
| Reforms | April 2026 contribution rise; October 2026 subrogation end |

### Grid pass (`ouch-legal`, checks 3–5 and 8)

- No banned word, no generalisation about an organisation; T03 states the trainee's request, not a judgement on the rule.
- Entities: only `mon-compte-formation` (already in the registry) and `france-competences` (new, public body). « OPCO », « DREETS » and « certificateur » stay unnamed categories, so T05, T06, T08 carry no entity.
- Hashtags name the context, never an entity.
- Sourcing rows for T04 and T09 added to the [sourcing sheet](../research/sourcing-sheet-entity-cards.md).
- T06 stays **unpublished** until the 1 October 2026 subrogation end is confirmed on an official text (ministère du Travail or Code du travail).

### Editor notes (`ouch-editeur-cartes`)

- Launch row for a trainer wave (5 cards, most recognisable in two seconds): T05, T03, T04, T07, T10. T01 and T02 for the catalogue; T06 as soon as confirmed; T08 and T09 for year-one trainers.
