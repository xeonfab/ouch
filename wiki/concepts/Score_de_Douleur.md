# Score de Douleur

> One-line TL;DR: the pain-score formula that ranks problems in the Terminal Entrepreneur, letting an entrepreneur find an already-validated market before building anything.

## What it is

Weighted score computed per problem: **45% volume of positive swipes + 35% conversion rate + 20% opt-in emails collected**. It's the single sorting/filtering key on the Terminal Entrepreneur dashboard.

## Why it matters for Ouch!/FixMyLife

It's the core mechanism turning raw swipe data into an entrepreneur-facing signal — the whole product thesis is that an entrepreneur can trust this number instead of guessing demand. It also gates the launch-stage traction signal: moving from Cercle 1 (freelances) to Cercle 2 (PME) requires 3 freelance problems with a Score de Douleur >70 **and** at least one returning entrepreneur.

## Current status

- 🟢 Live / in production — computed and shown in the Terminal Entrepreneur.
- 🔴 Not supported: AI-suggested business ideas clustering multiple high-score problems (reserved for v2 Pro, deliberately not built — too risky to show before real data volume).
